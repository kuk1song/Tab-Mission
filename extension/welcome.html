More tips

  • Type a few letters to find a tab, then press Enter to switch to it.
  • In Tab Mission, point at a tab and press the shortcut to jump to it.
  • When the site you are on has several tabs open, press Tab in the search box to see only its tabs.
  • Middle-click a tab, or click its ×, to close it.

Tip: pin Tab Mission to the toolbar (puzzle icon, then the pin) to open it with one click.

Tab Mission has no server: your tab list and searches stay in your browser.
