Tab Mission settings

Keyboard shortcut

Mode

Display area

Where a page cannot be covered, or does not respond, "The page only" opens a window of the same size instead.

Your five most recent tabs come first, then a panel for each site with two or more tabs, then the rest.

When Tab Mission opens

Press the shortcut twice to go back. When off, nothing is selected, so a second press closes Tab Mission.

Appearance

It follows your system unless you choose one here.

Behind the cards. Plain while there is no picture of the page yet, or when your system asks for less transparency.

Privacy

Cards show each page as you last saw it. The pictures stay on this computer. When off, Tab Mission takes none and deletes the ones it has; cards show the page's preview image or the site's icon.

Help

Every time when on; when off, only the first few times after installing.
