Tab Mission settings

Keyboard shortcut

When Tab Mission opens

Press the shortcut twice to go back. When off, nothing is selected, so a second press closes Tab Mission.

Your five most recent tabs come first, then a panel for each site with two or more tabs, then the rest. The panels stay where they are as you switch tabs.

Appearance

For the Tab Mission window. It follows your system unless you choose one here.

Behind the cards. In the grid it stays faint, so titles remain readable; with the cards spread out it shows clearly.

Every tab in view at once, the ones you used last larger. With too many tabs to tell apart, the window shows its grid.

Help

Every time when on; when off, only the first few times after installing.
