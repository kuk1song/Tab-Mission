Tab Mission settings

Keyboard shortcuts

When Tab Mission opens

Press the shortcut twice to go back. When off, nothing is selected, so a second press closes Tab Mission.

Your five most recent tabs come first, then a group for each site with several tabs, which switching tabs doesn't change. The rest come last.

Help

Every time when on; when off, only the first few times after installing.
